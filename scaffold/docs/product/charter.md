# Product charter

One or two paragraphs: why this product exists and what outcome it delivers for its users.

Stable, and rarely edited. Not a roadmap and not a glossary: what a term means lives
in [`../../CONTEXT.md`](../../CONTEXT.md), and what is being pursued this week lives
in `active-context.md` at the repository root. A feature spec's Outcome sentence
should be traceable to a sentence here; when it is not, either the charter is out of
date or the feature is.
